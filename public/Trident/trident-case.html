01 — The Insight

THE PROBLEM
WITH DIVERSITY

It’s incredibly hard to find matching donors for patients from diverse ethnic backgrounds. The NHS even searches internationally to find compatible bone marrow matches — but the donor pool remains critically small.

Most people don’t register because the process feels complex or time-consuming. The perception barrier is enormous. But the actual biology is already there — in every piece of chewing gum, in every strand of saliva.

The Core Insight
Trident’s sugar-free gum
already collects your DNA.
We just needed to use it.

Case Visual — Save Your Diversity With Trident

02 — The Idea

THE SIMPLEST
DONATION
EVER MADE

By integrating a secure, resealable plastic bag inside the gum’s packaging, we turned every pack into a bone marrow registration kit. The saliva left on the gum provides enough DNA for HLA typing — the process that matches donors to patients.

No needles. No clinics. No complicated forms. Just the gum you were already buying.

“What if the most powerful medical test
felt exactly like chewing gum?”

How it works

01
Chew it

Just like any other Trident. Sugar-free, peppermint. The DNA is already in your saliva.

02
Pack it

Reseal the used gum in the custom grip-seal inner pouch built into every pack.

03
Drop it

Leave the pack at a donation collection point in ASDA or Tesco. Done.
