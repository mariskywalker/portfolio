03 Approach

STRATEGIC
PRINCIPLES.

Three principles guided every decision across formats and contexts.

Recognition first

The identity had to read as PokerBros immediately, at any scale. Recognisable before attractive.

Clarity under density

Poker is information dense. The system imposed hierarchy, fast navigation, and low friction without flattening personality.

Bold, not aggressive

The category leans confrontational. PokerBros chose confidence with warmth. That distinction shaped colour, type, and illustration.
