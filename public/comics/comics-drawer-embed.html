01, Concept

THE DETAILS
THAT GLOW
IN THE DARK

Comics is a bar and entertainment venue in São Paulo built around three cultural pillars: comics, music, and stand-up comedy. The concept demanded a brand identity as immersive as the space itself, one that felt lived-in, layered, and unmistakably of its world.

The foundational idea was drawn from urban noir: dark, damp alleyways with rusted fire escapes and graffiti-covered brick walls, where secret places protect themselves from curious eyes.

"Dark alleys, neon signs, the smell of rain on concrete where the extraordinary hides behind the ordinary."

03, Space & Architecture

The architectural concept translated the brand narrative directly into space. The facade study drew from vintage Broadway marquees, a luminous COMICS sign in red, framed by incandescent bulbs, rusted iron structures, and deliberate imperfection.

Inside, three reference worlds converged: gritty urban texture, jazz-era club warmth, and immersive scenography. Iron, wood, and velvet.
