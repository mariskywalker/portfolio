01, Concept

THE DETAILS
THAT GLOW
IN THE DARK

Comics is a bar and entertainment venue in Sao Paulo built around three cultural pillars: comics, music, and stand-up comedy. The concept demanded a brand identity as immersive as the space itself, one that felt lived-in, layered, and unmistakably of its world.

The foundational idea was drawn from urban noir: dark, damp alleyways with rusted fire escapes and graffiti-covered brick walls, where secret places protect themselves from curious eyes. A world of details that glow in the darkness and open passages to an alternative reality.

"Dark alleys, neon signs, the smell of rain on concrete where the extraordinary hides behind the ordinary."

Design Principles

Strong & Direct

The identity was built to be bold without being loud. Inspired by legendary publishing houses and jazz club marks, strong, direct, and instantly memorable. No unnecessary decoration. Every line earns its place.

Elegant Decay

Industrial rawness, iron structures, rusted metals, exposed brick, balanced with designed lighting and artistic expression. The unfinished and the refined coexisting, as they do in the best underground venues.

Immersive Scenography

The space was conceived as a set, not a room. Every object, fixture, and material choice was an extension of the brand narrative, comics artifacts, repurposed instruments, collectibles that transform the venue into a living world.

Typography as Identity

The BAHN typeface, with its thick strokes and deliberate imperfections, became the backbone of the visual language. Its reference to letterpress printing and comic book fonts was intentional, direct, and deeply consistent.

03, Space & Architecture

NOW
SHOWING

The architectural concept translated the brand narrative directly into space. The facade study drew from vintage Broadway marquees, a luminous COMICS sign in red, framed by incandescent bulbs, rusted iron structures, and deliberate imperfection.

Inside, three reference worlds converged: the gritty urban texture of comics alleyways, the warmth of jazz-era club interiors, and the immersive scenography of a curated cultural space. Iron, wood, and velvet. Industrial rawness balanced by projected lighting and carefully chosen artistic interventions.
