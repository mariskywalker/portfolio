Sr. Comics — Brand Character
SR
Brand Character · Mascot
SR.
COMICS

Every great brand has a voice. Comics had a face.

Sr. Comics was conceived as the venue’s alter ego and mascot — the superhero of the space. Like Tato for PokerBros, he embodied the soul of the brand: noir, cultural, jazz-soaked, and unapologetically bold. A character that gives the identity personality beyond the logo.

  • Brand mascot & storytelling anchor
  • Cultural ambassador of the venue
  • Presence across print, signage & collateral
  • Identity extension beyond the mark
