01, Campaign

Santa stays home.
The Bunny steps up.

Christmas 2020. Brazil was navigating the pandemic, and Lacta, the country's most iconic chocolate brand, decided to meet the moment with a story. In partnership with David agency: Santa belongs to the risk group. He must stay home. Who will deliver the gifts?

The answer: the Easter Bunny steps in as Santa's helper for the very first time navigating snowy rooftops and chimneys with everything he's got.

"Although this is a fairytale, there is a very important message. Lacta is starting a movement that brings awareness about how magical Christmas is, even if we need to celebrate in a different way." Rafael Donato, VP Creative Director, David

02, Character Design

From sketch

The brief called for a character that felt like it belonged in a classic children's picture book, familiar enough to trust, expressive enough to carry the story. White, round, long earnest ears, and ludicrous features that remind you of childhood storytelling books.
